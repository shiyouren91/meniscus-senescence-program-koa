## Recommendation

The best first target for the current in silico manuscript is **Journal of Orthopaedic Translation**. It offers the strongest balance of impact factor, orthopaedic/translational scope, musculoskeletal readership, and tolerance for a validation-roadmap style manuscript.

The highest disease-field prestige option is **Osteoarthritis and Cartilage**, but it carries a higher risk because the manuscript lacks wet-lab, protein, spatial, or perturbation validation.

The strongest pure-bioinformatics option is **Genomics, Proteomics & Bioinformatics**, especially if the manuscript is reframed as a reproducible program-level omics workflow.

## Caution

Journal metrics and APCs can change. The shortlist records information visible on 2026-06-01 and should be rechecked immediately before submission.

Do not pursue Cell Communication and Signaling, International Journal of Biological Sciences, or Genes & Diseases as first choices unless additional validation is added or the manuscript is substantially reframed.
